Custom IR sensor module with 3D-printed enclosure

Analog circuit design, PCB fabrication, mechanical design

Portfolio CV (English) Lebenslauf (DE)

Overview

A custom infrared sensor module built end to end: schematic and single-layer PCB layout designed in Autodesk EAGLE around an LM358 op-amp, the board etched and hand-soldered, and a snap-fit enclosure modeled to the exact board dimensions and FDM 3D-printed.

What I did

  • Designed the custom schematic and single-layer PCB layout in EAGLE around the LM358 op-amp.
  • Fabricated the board: toner transfer, chemical etching, drilling.
  • Assembled and hand-soldered all through-hole components.
  • Modeled the snap-fit enclosure to the PCB dimensions and 3D-printed it (FDM).

Design and build

Tools and skills shown

Autodesk EAGLE (schematic and layout), PCB etching and through-hole soldering, 3D CAD modeling, FDM 3D printing.
