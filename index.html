04

Projects

Grouped by the kind of work, not by date, so related builds sit together. The first group is the work I was paid to do, the rest is my own.

A. Repair and restoration

Take a dead or misbehaving unit, find the fault at component level, put it back into service.

1Equipment restoration archive

K150 PIC programmers with dead USB recognition brought back to life; department PID control trainer units restored; damaged wiring-harness terminals on 5/2 solenoid valves and pressure sensors rebuilt. Also repaired development boards, sensor kits and power conversion circuits in daily workshop use.

B. Embedded and electronics builds

Boards designed and etched by hand, firmware on ESP and Arduino, enclosures modeled and printed to fit.

2Secondary keyboard macro pad and room lighting controller

A second USB keyboard isolated by hardware ID through the Interception driver, scripted with AutoHotkey v2 to run macros (OBS scene switching) and talk over USB serial to an Arduino that switches room lighting relays. Project page with working demo video →

3Custom IR sensor module with 3D-printed enclosure

LM358 op-amp circuit designed in EAGLE, etched and hand-soldered as a single-layer PCB, fitted in a snap-fit enclosure modeled and printed to the board dimensions. Project page with schematic and build photos →

4Custom PCB prototyping, EAGLE and bench etching

Single-sided boards with op-amp comparator circuits (LM339/LM358) and IRF540 MOSFET drivers: schematic capture, routing, toner transfer, FeCl3 etching, drilling and soldering.

5EMG bio-signal interface

Surface EMG muscle sensor modules wired to Arduino/ESP boards, capturing raw biopotential signals and plotting live voltage spikes over serial.

6Otto Ninja bipedal walking robot

Four-servo walking robot on an ESP8266 with custom C++ gait logic and a 3D-printed chassis. Adapted and modified from an existing open project, with AI assistance.

7Magnetic door alarm

Compact magnetic door alarm built for a friend: a reed switch and magnet on the door, about 10 m of wire running upstairs to a buzzer in their second apartment, all fitted in a custom-designed 3D-printed enclosure. It beeps intermittently whenever the door opens.

C. Software and automation

Tools I wrote because the manual version of the job was wasting someone's time, mine included.

8TCC grade management tool

Standalone PySimpleGUI and pandas desktop app built for the training center to record and process student evaluations across the pneumatics, electricity and hydraulics labs.

9Call-center shift and pay tracker

Mobile-first web app that tracks shifts automatically from a rotating schedule and shows live net earnings with night allowances, KPI bonuses and holiday multipliers. Single-file vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, offline-first, with payslip-based deduction calibration, notifications and JSON/CSV backup. Four versions shipped in one week, in daily personal use.

10Shift-swap matcher for colleagues

A Google Form and linked sheet that collect each person's shift and off days, plus a scheduled script that pairs up compatible swaps and notifies both people by email and WhatsApp the moment a match fits. Built with a colleague, now used by people on my team.

D. In progress

Designed and heading for a first print. Nothing is for sale yet.

11Pocket break buzzerPrototype

A pocket-sized break timer for call-center and customer-service agents, who all break at the same fixed times. Two-part 3D-printed case, tactile button and vibration motor, with gestures that match how a break actually works: double-press to start a break, hold one second for lunch, press to stop, hold four seconds to switch an early warning three or eight minutes before the end, felt as one or two buzzes. Case designed, STLs finalised, first prints ordered; assembly next.
